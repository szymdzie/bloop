Support

Something not working, or an idea for a new toy? Write to chochoapps@gmail.com. We answer every message.

My purchase did not arrive

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I turn tilt control on or off?

Settings → Tilt control. With it on, tilting the phone tilts the water in the toy.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Progress is saved on the device (there is no account). A full device backup (iCloud or computer) includes it, and Premium can always be brought back with Restore purchases.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. BLOOP speaks English and Polish.

Can I start over?

Settings → Reset progress erases stars, records and pearls. Premium stays.

Please include your device model and the game version (Settings, at the bottom) when you report a problem.
