Privacy Policy

BLOOP: Water Ring Toss · last updated 3 October 2026

BLOOP collects no personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

  • No account. There is no sign-in and no registration.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The game contains no advertising or analytics tools and never asks to track you.
  • Saved on your device only. Your progress, stars, pearls, unlocked looks and settings are stored on the device. Deleting the game deletes them. Settings → Reset progress erases your progress (Premium stays).
  • Purchases. Premium and pearl packs are sold through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game remembers on the device what you bought, and Settings → Restore purchases asks the store again.
  • Motion sensors. Tilt control reads the device's motion sensors while you play, only to move the water in the game. The readings are never stored or sent anywhere. You can turn tilt control off in Settings.
  • Works offline. The game makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Crash reports. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may pass us anonymous crash reports. They contain no personal data.
  • Children. We collect no data from anyone, including children.
  • Changes. If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
